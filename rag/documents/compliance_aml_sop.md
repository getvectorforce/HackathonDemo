# SOP: Anti-Money Laundering (AML), OFAC Sanctions & Regulatory Disclosures

## Section 1: FinCEN Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) Triggers
- A Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) must be filed within **30 calendar days** after the date of initial detection of facts that may constitute a basis for filing.
- Mandatory filing triggers:
  1. Any known or suspected criminal violation involving an employee or insider, regardless of amount.
  2. Suspected transactions involving **$5,000 or more** where a potential suspect can be identified.
  3. Suspected transactions involving **$25,000 or more** regardless of whether a suspect is identified.
  4. Multiple structured or irregular cross-border transfers exhibiting mule account behavior, smurfing, or automated layering.

## Section 2: Currency Transaction Report (CTR) Requirements
- Financial institutions must file a FinCEN Currency Transaction Report (CTR) for each deposit, withdrawal, exchange of currency, or other payment or transfer by, through, or to the institution that involves currency of **more than $10,000** in a single business day.

## Section 3: OFAC Sanctions Compliance
- Every international destination entity, BIC/SWIFT code, and foreign recipient name must be verified against the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list.
- If a match or high-confidence fuzzy match is detected, funds must be blocked immediately and reported to OFAC within **10 business days**.
