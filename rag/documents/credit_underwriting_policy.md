# Credit & Underwriting Policy: Dispute Adjustments & Payment Protections

## Section 1: Protection of Standing Loans and Auto-Debits
- When a customer account balance is depleted below the minimum required balance due to an unconfirmed or disputed debit transaction:
  - **Grace Period Application**: Automatic 14-day hold on any scheduled auto-debits for internal loan products (auto loans, personal lines of credit, mortgages).
  - **Overdraft Forgiveness**: Overdraft interest and per-item return fees triggered by the fraudulent debit are subject to 100% waiver.
  - **Credit Bureau Non-Derogatory Flag**: Under Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) rules, while a transaction is formally disputed under Regulation E, the institution will NOT report a 30-day delinquency to Equifax, Experian, or TransUnion.

## Section 2: Temporary Credit Extension
- If the customer requires liquidity before the formal 10-day provisional credit window elapses (e.g., immediate essential living expenses or pending mortgage payment):
  - Underwriting can grant an instant **Bridge Discretionary Credit Line** up to $3,000 at 0% APR.
  - This bridge credit is automatically absorbed and cleared once the dispute resolution or chargeback recovery concludes.
